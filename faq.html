Ordering & payment

How can I pay for my order?

We accept M-Pesa (Lipa na M-Pesa), cash on pickup or delivery, and bank transfer. Orders paid by M-Pesa or bank transfer are confirmed after we verify the payment (the M-Pesa SMS code or the bank advice). Cash orders are paid when you pick up or when delivery is made.

Delivery & pickup

Where do you deliver, and where can I pick up?

We currently serve Embu and Garissa only, for both pickup and home delivery. Embu branch: Moi Avenue, Embu Town, next to Embu Level 5 Hospital. Garissa branch: Garissa Town Center, opposite the County Government Offices. Both branches are open Monday to Saturday, 8AM to 6PM.

Tracking your order

How do I track my order?

Open the Track Order page and enter your order number (it looks like ORD-20241104-0001) together with the email or phone number you used when you placed the order. We ask for this so only you can see your order.

Parts & fitment

How do I know a part fits my car?

Descriptions and fitment information are there to help you choose, but always check compatibility with your vehicle (make, model, year and engine). If you are unsure, ask us before ordering: send your vehicle details through the contact form or contact us by phone or WhatsApp.

I can't find the part I need. What can I do?

Tell us what you need using the contact form, or by phone or WhatsApp. For broken or rare parts you can also ask for a custom 3D-printed part on our 3D Printing page.

3D printing

How does custom 3D printing work?

Go to the 3D Printing page and fill in the request form: your vehicle, a description of the part, any measurements you have, and photos or 3D files (STL, OBJ) if you have them. You can pick a material or let us suggest one. We print in PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU (flexible).
