Frequently Asked Questions

What parts can you 3D print?

We can print interior trim, clips, brackets, handles, knobs, custom mounts, and decorative parts. We CANNOT print safety-critical components like brake parts, steering components, or structural elements.

How long does it take?

Most parts are ready in 24-48 hours. Complex or large parts may take 2-3 days. We'll give you an exact timeframe with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Prices start from KSh 500 for small clips and go up based on size and material. Most parts cost 50-70% less than OEM equivalents.

What if it doesn't fit?

We'll work with you to get it right! Minor adjustments are free. We recommend verifying dimensions before ordering when possible.

Is it strong enough?

Yes! We use industrial-grade materials. PETG and ABS prints are very durable and suitable for automotive use in non-structural applications.

Do you ship nationwide?

Currently we serve Embu and Garissa with pickup/delivery. Shipping to other locations can be arranged for larger orders.
